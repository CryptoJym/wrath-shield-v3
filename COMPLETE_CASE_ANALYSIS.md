## 📋 UTAH CODE §81-9-209 - RELOCATION REQUIREMENTS

### **60-Day Notice Requirement**:
**Statutory Text**: Relocating parent must provide written notice 60 days before proposed move to:
- Non-relocating parent
- Any person with court-ordered parent-time

**CRITICAL QUESTION FOR ZACK**:
- When did Destiny provide this written notice to James?
- Sep 12 court filing = only 39 days before Oct 21 hearing
- Did she give informal notice before court filing?
- **If she failed to provide proper 60-day notice, this is a statutory violation**

### **Factors Court MUST Consider** (Best Interests Standard):
1. ✅ **Reason for relocation** → What did she say in Oct 1 affidavit?
2. ✅ **Impact on child's education** → Canyon Grove disruption
3. ✅ **Impact on child's social life** → Friends at school?
4. ✅ **Impact on emotional well-being** → Separation from daily father involvement
5. ✅ **Child's relationship with each parent** → James's active involvement
6. ✅ **Child's need for stability** → Recent modification + current school enrollment
7. ✅ **Ability to maintain relationship with non-relocating parent** → Impossible if 150+ miles
8. ✅ **Costs/difficulty in exercising parent-time** → Who pays for 300-mile round trips?
9. ✅ **Economic resources of both parents** → Can she afford frequent travel? Can James?

### **Potential Court Orders**:
- **If relocation NOT in best interests**: Court can DENY relocation
- **If she relocates anyway**: Court may ORDER CHANGE OF CUSTODY to James
