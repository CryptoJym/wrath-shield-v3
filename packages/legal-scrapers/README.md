# Legal Advocate AI - Strategic Case Guardian

**Mission**: Act as an experienced family‑law attorney's analytical brain—continuously ingesting the full case history, spotting risks, and surfacing the right document, fact, or next action at the moment you and your attorney need it—while keeping everything private on your Mac.

**THIS TOOL IS NOT LEGAL ADVICE.** This system provides legal information and workflow assistance only. It does not replace an attorney and should never present itself as legal counsel.
