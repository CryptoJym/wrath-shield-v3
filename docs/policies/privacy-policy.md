# Privacy Policy (ReWrite LLC) - Provisional

**Organization:** ReWrite LLC  
**Contact:** privacy@jamesbrady.org (monitored)  
**Effective date:** 2025-11-22

## 1. What we collect
- Identity: name, email, authentication data (passkeys/TOTP metadata).
- Connected sources (when user authorizes): emails, calendar items, messages, lifelog data, financial transactions (via user-consented APIs like Plaid), task/project data.
- Usage and telemetry: app interactions, execution logs (redacted), device/browser metadata.

## 2. How we use data
- Provide the EA/automation services, generate tasks/actions, and surface insights.
- Improve service quality and security (anomaly detection, abuse prevention).
- Support and troubleshooting.

## 3. Legal basis (if applicable)
- Consent (for connected sources and processing).
- Legitimate interest (service operation and security).

## 4. Sharing
- No selling of personal data.
- Third-party processors only as needed (e.g., cloud hosting, auth provider, vector DB) under DPAs and least privilege.
- Disclosures required by law or to protect rights/safety.

## 5. Retention & deletion
- Retain only as long as needed for the purpose or legal obligation.
- User-initiated deletion honored; connected-source tokens revoked on request.
- Backups follow same retention and are purged on schedule.

## 6. Security
- Encryption in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest.
- MFA for admin access; RBAC; audit logs for privileged actions.
- Secrets managed securely; periodic security reviews.

## 7. User rights
- Access, correction, deletion, and export on request (subject to law).
- Revoke access to connected sources at any time.

## 8. Cookies/Tracking
- Minimal functional cookies for session/auth; no third-party ad tracking.

## 9. Children
- Not directed to children under 16; accounts for minors require guardian consent.

## 10. Contact
- privacy@jamesbrady.org for privacy requests; security@jamesbrady.org for security reports.
