# Agent Asset Prompts (Continuity & Reality Based)

These prompts are designed to generate 16-bit RPG pixel art characters that reflect the actual code and data reality of the Wrath Shield v3 system.

## Existing Agents

### Legal Advocate
**Context**: Manages `legal_actions` table, interacts with MyCase and Gmail.
**Prompt**: 16-bit pixel art of a stern High Inquisitor or Cleric. They hold a glowing scroll titled 'MyCase' in one hand and a gavel made of golden light in the other. Robes are deep blue with gold trim. Background is a faint grid of legal text.
**Status**: ACTIVE

### Finance Analyst
**Context**: Manages `finance_transactions`, `cycles`, and `recoveries` (WHOOP data).
**Prompt**: 16-bit pixel art of a Master Treasurer or Alchemist. They are surrounded by floating gold coins and green data streams (representing cash flow). They hold a ledger with the WHOOP logo faintly visible on the cover. Green and gold color scheme.
**Status**: ACTIVE

### Project Maestro (PM)
**Context**: Syncs Motion tasks and GitHub issues (`.taskmaster` directory).
**Prompt**: 16-bit pixel art of a Field Commander or Bard. They wield a baton that directs floating task cards (Motion & GitHub icons). They wear armor that looks like a mix of plate mail and futuristic tech. Purple and silver color scheme.
**Status**: ACTIVE

### Comms Scout
**Context**: Ingests Gmail and iMessage, filters noise.
**Prompt**: 16-bit pixel art of a Ranger or Rogue. They wear a hood and have a glowing visor (scanning messages). They hold a datapad that emits a holographic envelope. Dark grey and neon blue color scheme.

### Executive Assistant (EA)
**Context**: Manages Calendar and Travel.
**Prompt**: 16-bit pixel art of a Royal Vizier or Chronomancer. They stand before a floating clock face (Calendar). They hold a quill that writes on the air. Elegant robes of white and gold.

### Relationship Manager
